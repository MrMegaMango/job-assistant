# Levels.fyi compensation coverage

The `/companies` directory contains employers with a publicly readable Levels.fyi annual total compensation figure of at least USD 300,000, across any job family and location. Figures are third-party reported compensation or estimates, including salary, stock and bonus. They are examples, not advertised salaries, offers, or a claim that every opening pays that amount. Employer-posted job compensation remains separate.

The snapshot in `src/lib/server/data/levels-companies.json` was checked on September 21, 2026 (America/Los_Angeles). Each company has a direct evidence URL and the reported role, level and location where available. A source update date is only populated when it was visible; it is distinct from our check date. Some company summaries describe a role/level median, while role-specific FAQs can include larger individual packages. One qualifying example is enough for inclusion. No exchange-rate conversion is inferred.

Coverage is incomplete. The official [company sitemap](https://www.levels.fyi/sitemaps/companies-sitemap.xml) exposed 65,028 salary-summary profiles across 80 sitemap shards. A broad retrieval attempt produced mostly empty HTTP 200 bodies and was stopped. Those responses were never treated as below-threshold evidence. Readable company and role pages were checked separately; successfully verified companies were removed from the unresolved count. The UI distinguishes researched companies from the full index and shows unresolved coverage. A complete [Levels.fyi data export or API](https://www.levels.fyi/api-access/) would be needed to establish exhaustive coverage of underlying submissions.

`sourceKey` is an explicit reviewed `provider:boardToken` mapping to a live public employer feed. It does not use fuzzy company names: Flex the manufacturer is different from the rent-payment company, and TOAST clothing is different from Toast restaurant software. D. E. Shaw's duplicate profiles are represented once. Companies without a supported feed remain visible with a Levels.fyi openings link; that link does not imply their jobs are synchronized.

To extend the snapshot, add only cited, qualifying annual USD evidence; preserve unresolved cases and deduplicate employer aliases. Verify a new feed's public endpoint and employer identity before adding it to `source-catalog.ts`, then assign the explicit key. Update check counts and date, run `npm run ci`, and visually verify `/companies` and `/sources` on desktop and mobile before deployment. Do not put account exports, individual salary submissions, credentials, or candidate details in this public snapshot.
